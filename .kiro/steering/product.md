# PaintShop Industrial Telemetry & Quality Dashboard

## Product Overview
An enterprise-grade, real-time telemetry and quality dashboard designed for industrial paint shops. The system ingests live sensor data (temperature and humidity), correlates it with coating defects, tracks system alerts, and calculates an ongoing Coating Quality Index (CQI).

## Core Purpose
- Real-time monitoring of industrial sensor data for paint shop environments
- Quality assurance through CQI (Coating Quality Index) calculation
- Alert management for system anomalies and sensor faults
- Data correlation between environmental conditions and coating defects

## Key User Workflows
1. **Real-time Monitoring**: Operators view live telemetry from paint shop sensors
2. **Quality Control**: Quality engineers track CQI scores and defect correlation
3. **System Administration**: Admins configure sensors, manage alerts, and oversee system health
4. **Data Analysis**: Engineers analyze historical trends and export reports

## Business Value
- Prevent coating defects through environmental monitoring
- Reduce material waste by maintaining optimal conditions
- Improve quality control with data-driven CQI scoring
- Enable proactive maintenance through alert systems
